## 1. Complete Dimensional & Material Specifications (All 6 Models)

### A. Structural Spine & Neck Geometry
* **Construction:** 1-Piece Monocoque High-Modulus Prepreg Carbon Fiber (`Toray T700 / T800` unidirectional neck layup + 3K twill shear web) cured around a closed-cell **Rohacell 71 IG-F** structural foam core, reinforced with two internal `6 mm × 12 mm` pultruded carbon I-beams flanking a dual-action Grade-5 Titanium truss rod.
* **Surface Finish:** Solid **Matte Satin Stealth Obsidian** (2K ceramic-infused polyurethane matte clear over black-pigmented epoxy primer, `1500-grit` velvety neck finish).
* **Fretboard:** **Richlite Black Diamond** (`6.0 mm` thick), **16" to 20" compound radius** (20" flat on 7/8-string models), `2.5 mm` glowing **Luminlay** side markers.
* **Frets:** **24x Jescar FW57110-S Stainless Steel Jumbo** (`2.79 mm` wide × `1.45 mm` tall) + **1x Jescar FW57110-S Stainless Zero-Fret** (`0.8 mm–1.0 mm` shred action at 12th fret).

### B. Hardware, Electronics & Tool-Free Wing Mechanism Specs
1. **Rear Protective Tuner Guard (U-Cradle):**
   * **Material:** 5-axis CNC-machined **Grade-5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V)**, bead-blasted matte finish, bolted to the rear carbon spine via 4x `M5` stainless socket bolts threaded into titanium barrel inserts.
   * **Clearance:** Extends `12.0 mm` past the rear of the tuning barrels and forms a `4.0 mm` lower protective floor underneath the barrels, with a scalloped open top window for finger tuning.
2. **Tool-Free Wing Coupling ("Click-Lock" Dovetail System):**
   * **Receivers:** Two flush CNC-machined `15°` tapered dovetail pockets (`32 mm` long × `14 mm` wide × `8 mm` deep) embedded into the bass side (at frets 15–18) and treble side (at the bridge/U-guard junction).
   * **Wings:**
     * **3A Upper Horn Blade:** Hollow-milled 6061-T6 aluminum or Grade-5 Titanium (`145 g`), locates the front Schaller S-Lock strap pin directly over the 12th-fret center of gravity.
     * **3B Skeletal Lower Leg Rail:** CNC-skeletonized frame (`185 g`), forms the seated thigh contour and classical left-leg hook.
   * **Locking Pin:** Captive spring-loaded **quarter-turn radial ball-lock pin** (`Ø 6.0 mm` hardened 17-4PH stainless steel shaft with red anodized knurled aluminum head, rated to `1,800 N / 400 lbs` shear load). Push and turn `90°` to lock/unlock with zero play.
3. **Bridge & String Lock:**
   * **Guitars (6/7/8):** **Hantug Custom Headless Floating Tremolo** or **Tan Bridges TT-Series / Hipshot Headless Tremolo** (`10.5 mm` string spacing) + CNC Titanium Zero-Fret String Lock.
   * **Basses (6/7/8):** **ABM 3901** or **Nova** individual single-string headless monorail bridge-tuners (`16.5 mm` string spacing for fast technical playability).
4. **Electronics (Single Pickup, Side-Edge Controls):**
   * **Guitar Pickup:** **Fishman Fluence Modern Ceramic** (or **Lundgren M6/M7/M8** passive) soapbar humbucker mounted `12 mm` from bridge saddle.
   * **Bass Pickup:** **Nordstrand Big Blademan / EMG DCX** soapbar.
   * **Controls:** Flush side-edge **knurled titanium thumbwheel potentiometer** (`500k` Bourns push-push pot for Voice 1 / Voice 2 coil split) + recessed side-mounted **Neutrik NJ3FP6C-BAG locking 1/4" jack**.

### C. Self-Contained Carbon Capsule & CNC Backpack Side-Latch Specs
1. **Self-Contained Carbon Tube Capsule:**
   * **Construction:** Autoclave/vacuum-infused `2.0 mm` wall 3K twill carbon fiber clamshell case with waterproof anodized aluminum tongue-and-groove valence, continuous piano hinge, and 2x flush butterfly latches.
   * **Guitar Capsule Outer Dimensions:** **`745 mm` (29.3") Long × `105 mm` (4.13") Wide × `82 mm` (3.23") Deep** (`Weight: 1.15 kg / 2.53 lbs`).
   * **Bass Capsule Outer Dimensions:** **`965 mm` (38.0") Long × `138 mm` (5.43") Wide × `85 mm` (3.35") Deep** (`Weight: 1.55 kg / 3.41 lbs`).
   * **Internal CNC EVA Foam Layout:**
     * **Bottom Tray (`45 mm` deep):** Holds the 1-piece bare guitar/bass stick at full string tension.
     * **Top Lid Tray (`35 mm` deep above the narrow neck, `y = 280 mm to 720 mm`):** Holds the detached **3A Upper Horn**, **3B Lower Leg Rail**, and **Tremolo Arm** behind a `10 mm` protective foam divider wall so metal never touches the fretboard.
2. **CNC Side-Latch Backpack Coupling:**
   * **Backpack Frame:** `520 × 320 × 155 mm` (`20.5" × 12.6" × 6.1"`) ballistic `1680D` Cordura + Hypalon dual-laptop backpack with an internal `3.0 mm` carbon-composite frame sheet.
   * **Side Clamps:** Two CNC-machined **7075-T6 Aluminum cam-locking jaws** bolted directly through the internal frame sheet on both left and right sides (`260 mm` vertical center-to-center spacing), gripping two flush-mounted stainless mating collars on the Carbon Capsule.
